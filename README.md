This project demonstrates an **end-to-end sales data analytics workflow** using Python, including data cleaning, exploratory data analysis (EDA), visualization, and automated Excel report generation.

The workflow covers:

- Data cleaning

This project answers several business questions:

- Which category generated the highest profit?
- Does discount affect profitability?
- Which sub-category contributed the most sales?

---

# 📊 Business Insights

## 1️⃣ Total Sales by Region

![Sales by Region](Sales%20by%20Region.png)

### Insight

- West generated the highest total sales.
- East ranked second.
- South recorded the lowest sales.

---

## 2️⃣ Profit by Category

![Profit by Category](profit_by_category.png)

### Insight

Technology products generated the highest overall profit, indicating stronger profitability compared to Furniture and Office Supplies.

---

## 3️⃣ Relationship Between Discount and Profit

![Discount vs Profit](discount_vs_profit.png)

### Insight

Higher discounts generally resulted in lower profits.

Several transactions with discounts above 50% even produced negative profits.

---

## 4️⃣ Top Sales Sub Category

![Top Sub Category](top_sub_category.png)

### Insight

The top-performing sub-categories contributed a significant proportion of overall sales.
